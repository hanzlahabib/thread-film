# Sound

A film made with the engine has sound without any per-stop work: sound effects are cued from the schedule (when the head leaves, lands, and when rings, labels and the end card appear) and a music bed plays under it. This file is the cue table, the timing rules and the knobs.

## The cue table

| Cue | When it fires | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `whooshLong` | Every flight that crosses to another screen | `whoosh-long.wav` (1.6 s) | Peaks at departure + 40% of the flight; starts a few frames before the head leaves. Dips the music. |
| `whoosh` | A hop inside a screen longer than 220 world px | `whoosh-soft.wav` (0.7 s) | Same peak rule. Shorter hops only tick. |
| `tick` | Every landing, on the arrival frame | `tick.wav` | Soft sine/triangle with a fast decay. |
| `ring` | When a stop's ring pulses (stops without a `ring` stay silent) | `ring.wav` | One frame early; its soft attack lands on the pulse. |
| `pop` | When a label opens, 5 frames after landing | `pop.wav` | |
| `swap` | 3 frames after a landing that carried a chip | `swap.wav` | The chip turning into the element. |
| `riser` | Into the closing pull-back, peaking 14 frames into it | `riser.wav` (1.2 s) | Dips the music. |
| `chime` | When the end card starts to fade in | `chime.wav` (2.2 s) | Two frames early. Dips the music. |

Every file is generated by `scripts/make-audio.mjs` and committed under `public/audio/`. `src/engine/audio-manifest.json` lists the files, their lengths and the second at which each effect peaks, and the engine reads the timing from it.

## Timing rules
- **Align the loudest moment, not the file start.** A whoosh starts early enough that its peak (listed in the manifest) lands at departure + 40% of the flight; the head leaves fast, so that is when it is travelling fastest.
- **A tick lands exactly on arrival.** Anything with a soft attack (ring, chime) starts one to two frames before the picture so it reads as in sync. Frames are 33 ms; do not shift cues by more than the table does.
- **Every flight whooshes, every landing ticks.** There is no per-stop switch for this. If a film feels too busy, lower `sfx.volume` or mute one cue; do not delete cues stop by stop.
- **Effects never overlap into mush.** The cues of one landing (tick, ring, pop) are different sounds a few frames apart. A new effect belongs in the cue table, not on a stop.

## Mix rules
- **Never louder than narration.** The music ducks to about a third of its level while a line is spoken (a few frames of ramp each side) and dips to about 70% under a big effect (`whooshLong`, `riser`, `chime`). Effects drop to 60% while a line is spoken.
- **Defaults:** music 0.5, sfx 0.9 (about -17.7 LUFS on the demo film with a -2.2 dBFS peak; social platforms normalise toward -14 LUFS, so this is already on the quiet side, never lower it for a film without narration), both as Remotion volume (0..1). Every effect file is normalised to -3 dBFS and every bed to about -14 LUFS (peak -6 dBFS) before these are applied.
- **A film without narration** measures about -24 LUFS integrated at the defaults (peaks about -8 dBFS): quiet by design, because the sound is meant to sit under a viewer's own device volume. Raise `musicVolume` to about 0.3 and `sfx.volume` to about 0.8 for a loud autoplay feed, and keep peaks below -1 dBFS.
- **The music fades in over about 0.7 s and out over the last 1.5 s,** across the end card, and loops on its own length if the film is longer than the bed.

## Mood follows the brand
`theme.audio.mood` picks the bed so the sound follows the look. Default `calm`.

| Mood | Bed | Pace | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `calm` | warm pad, slow plucks, soft delay | 60 bpm, 32 s | most products, B2B, finance, wellness |
| `driven` | steady pulse, bass, soft kick, arpeggio | 120 bpm, 32 s | launches, dev tools, anything fast |
| `minimal` | quiet drone, a few bell notes | 80 bpm, 36 s | premium, design-led, editorial |

All three are in D major / B minor, as are the effects, so a bed and an effect never clash.

## Options (`journey.audio`)
```ts
audio: {
  music: 'driven',            // 'calm' | 'driven' | 'minimal' | 'audio/mine.mp3' | false. Default: the theme's mood
  musicVolume: 0.5,           // 0..1
  sfx: {volume: 0.5, overrides: {tick: 'audio/my-tick.wav', ring: false}},  // or true / false
  duck: true,                 // default true
}
```
- A file path is relative to `public/`. A custom bed is measured at render time and looped; cut it at a bar line so the seam is clean.
- `overrides` maps a cue name to a replacement file, or `false` to mute that cue. The timing rules above still apply to a replacement: if its peak is not at the start of the file, trim the file so it is.
- The legacy `music: {file, volume}` on the journey still works and is used when `audio.music` is not set.
- `pnpm run validate` checks that every referenced file exists and that volumes are in range.

## Checking sound
Frames cannot show sound. Render audio alone to check it quickly (`remotion render src/index.ts film-16x9 out/audio.wav --codec=wav`), then measure with ffmpeg: peak below -1 dBFS, no digital silence in the middle, loudness around the numbers above, and a loudness timeline around a flight, a landing and the end card. State plainly that this is measurement, not listening, unless someone listened.
